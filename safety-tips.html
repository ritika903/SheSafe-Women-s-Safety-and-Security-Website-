Women's Safety Guidelines

1. Personal Safety

  • Always be aware of your surroundings.
  • Keep emergency contacts saved.
  • Avoid sharing personal information with strangers.
  • Trust your instincts in uncomfortable situations.

2. Online Safety

  • Never share your passwords.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Do not share private photos with strangers.

3. Travel Safety

  • Inform family before travelling.
  • Use trusted transportation.
  • Share your journey details with someone you trust.
  • Keep your phone charged.

4. Home Safety

  • Always lock doors and windows.
  • Do not open doors to unknown people.
  • Keep emergency numbers nearby.
  • Inform trusted neighbours about emergencies.

Remember

Your safety is your right. Stay alert, stay confident and stay safe!
