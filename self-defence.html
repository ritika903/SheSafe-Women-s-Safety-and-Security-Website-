Why is Self Defence Important?

Self-defence helps women protect themselves in dangerous situations. It also improves confidence and awareness.

Basic Self Defence Tips

  1. Stay alert and observe your surroundings.
  2. Maintain a safe distance from suspicious people.
  3. Use a loud voice to attract attention.
  4. Move towards crowded and well-lit places.
  5. Try to escape from dangerous situations.
  6. Ask for help whenever necessary.

Important Safety Precautions

  • Attend professional self-defence classes.
  • Learn basic defensive movements from trained instructors.
  • Keep emergency numbers saved.
  • Trust your instincts.

Self Defence Awareness

Every woman should know basic safety techniques. Awareness, confidence and quick decision-making can help in difficult situations.

Remember: Your first priority should always be to escape and reach a safe place.
