What To Do During An Emergency?

  1. Stay calm and move towards a safe place.
  2. Call 112 if you need immediate emergency assistance.
  3. Inform a trusted family member or friend.
  4. Share your location with someone you trust if safe to do so.
  5. Follow instructions given by emergency responders.

Important Message

Never hesitate to ask for help. Your safety and life are important.
