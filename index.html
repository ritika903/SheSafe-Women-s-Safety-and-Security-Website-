Emergency Help

Emergency Helpline Numbers

  • Police Emergency: 112
  • Women Helpline: 181
  • Ambulance: 108
  • Child Helpline: 1098

Important Safety Tips

  1. Always inform someone before travelling alone.
  2. Keep emergency contacts saved in your phone.
  3. Avoid sharing personal information with strangers.
  4. Stay aware of your surroundings.
  5. Use trusted transportation services.

About SheSafe

SheSafe is a women's safety awareness website designed to educate women about personal safety, emergency support and self-defence.
